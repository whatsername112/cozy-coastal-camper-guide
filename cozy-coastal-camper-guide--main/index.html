💡 Tips & Tricks

A few helpful camper tips that answer some of the most common questions and make your stay even more comfortable.

💡 Bedroom Lights

The bedroom wall switch only controls the blue LED accent light above the bed.

The ceiling lights are push lights. Simply press the center of each light to turn it on or off.

⚡ Resetting the Outlets

If several electrical outlets suddenly stop working, don't worry—it usually isn't a power outage.

Simply press the RESET button on the bathroom outlet shown above. This normally restores power to the other outlets throughout the camper.

❄️ Keeping the Camper Cool

RV air conditioners work a little differently than those in most homes. During hot Florida days, these simple habits make a big difference:

  • Keep the entry door closed whenever possible.
  • Keep all windows closed while the air conditioner is running.
  • Keep the bathroom roof vent closed while the air conditioner is running.

Even leaving a door, window, or roof vent open for just a minute allows cool air to escape and warm, humid air to enter, making it much harder for the camper to stay comfortable.

🌙 For a Better Night's Sleep

Before going to bed, close the large ceiling A/C vent in the main living area.

  • 😴 Makes the air conditioner noticeably quieter.
  • ❄️ Redirects more cool air to the bedroom and bunk area.
  • 🌡️ Helps the sleeping areas cool down faster and stay more comfortable.

If you'd like more airflow in the living room during the day, simply reopen the large ceiling vent.

🚐 Living in Your Camper

A few helpful tips about everyday camper living to help make your stay as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

🚽 Toilet & Septic System

The camper uses a small RV septic system, which is much more sensitive than a household plumbing system.

  • Flush only the toilet paper provided in the bathroom.
  • Please do not flush wipes, feminine products, paper towels, or any other items.
  • If you need additional toilet paper during your stay, just send me a message and I'll be happy to provide more.

Helpful Tip: Before using the toilet, press the foot pedal halfway to add a little water to the bowl. This helps reduce odors from the holding tank when you flush.

You'll also notice a roof vent with an exhaust fan in the bathroom. While it's great for removing humidity after a shower, it's best to turn the fan off while flushing, as it can pull odors up from the holding tank.

💧 Water

The camper is connected to city water, so you can use the sinks, shower, and toilet normally.

If you happen to lose water or notice unusually low water pressure, please send me a message as soon as possible so I can get it taken care of.

🛏️ Extra Sleeping Space

Need a little more room? Both the couch and the dinette can easily be converted into beds.

Couch Bed

  1. Lift the front edge of the couch.
  2. Pull the couch outward.
  3. Lay it flat.

Flat sheets and extra blankets are stored in the cabinet beneath the TV.

Dinette Bed

  1. Remove the seat cushions.
  2. Lift the table straight up.
  3. Remove the support poles.
  4. Place the tabletop onto the black support ledges.
  5. Replace the cushions to create the mattress.

If you have any trouble converting either bed, just send me a message—I'm always happy to help.

🔥 Propane Grill

A propane grill is available for your enjoyment during your stay.

  • The push-button igniter is currently not working. A long grill lighter is located in the silverware drawer inside the camper. You'll nee to turn on the burner and manually light it.
  • A propane tank is attached to the grill for your convenience; however, the propane level is not guaranteed. If the tank is empty or runs out during your stay, guests are responsible for exchanging or refilling it if they wish to continue using the grill.
  • Please do not disconnect or use the camper's propane supply for the grill. The camper's propane is reserved for the RV appliances.
  • Please ensure the grill is completely turned off after each use and never leave it unattended while lit.

I'm unfortunately not much of a griller myself, so I may not be the best person for grilling tips or troubleshooting. If you're unfamiliar with propane grills, I recommend looking up a quick tutorial before using it.
