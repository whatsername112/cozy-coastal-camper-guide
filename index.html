Arrival

Welcome! Park in front of the camper door. Your key is located in the front exterior compartment. Please close the compartment after retrieving the key.

Guest Guide

Use this guide to find information about lights, A/C, bathroom, kitchen, grill, pets, troubleshooting, and checkout.

  • Bedroom ceiling lights are push-button lights.
  • Close the large A/C vent at night for quieter sleeping and better airflow.
  • Bathroom GFCI reset can restore outlets.
  • First aid kit is above the TV.

Pets

Pet supplies are available for guests who paid the pet fee. Please clean up after your pet.

Checkout

  • Turn lights off.
  • Set A/C to 82° on Auto.
  • Leave the door unlocked and return the key.
